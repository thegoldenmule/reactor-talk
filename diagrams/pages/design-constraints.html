Local-first
Data and execution live on the user's machine. Every peer is authoritative. Work continues offline.
Rules out
✗A privileged server copy that "wins"
Forces
→Command sourcing: ship intent, replay it locally
Append-only
Operations are written to append-only backends (Swarm, Hypercore). History is never edited.
Rules out
✗Reordering or rewriting past operations
Forces
→Create new operations instead of editing old ones
Audit trail
Every Action is signed by its author and every Operation is hash-chained to the last. Who changed what is always verifiable.
Rules out
✗Altering another user's signed operation (OT)
Forces
→Replay Actions verbatim; new Operations only
